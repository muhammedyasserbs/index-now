Privacy Policy

IndexFlow · Effective date: October 14, 2026 · Last updated: October 14, 2026

Short version: IndexFlow runs inside your browser. It requires no account, sends no data to the developer’s servers, and does not sell or share data with third parties.

1. Scope

This policy describes how the IndexFlow extension for Google Chrome handles data. It applies to the extension only. It does not apply to Google’s websites or to the sitemap websites you enter.

2. Data the extension handles

The extension does not collect personal information and does not create accounts. The following data is stored locally on your device using Chrome’s storage API:

  • Your settings: the sitemap URL used, the indexing mode, and the delay between requests.
  • The queue: the URLs you added, the status of each URL, and its processing attempts.
  • A history of URLs previously requested, to avoid submitting them twice.
  • The activity log and daily quota information shown in the panel.

Chrome’s session storage is also used for the current run state. It is cleared when the browser closes.

3. How the extension uses its permissions

  • Search Console tab: the extension reads page status and submits indexing requests only on Google Search Console pages, using the session you are signed in with. It does not operate on other Google sites.
  • Sitemaps: the extension asks the browser to fetch the sitemap you enter, and any child sitemaps it references, directly from that site. These requests are sent without cookies.
  • Notifications: to alert you when the queue finishes or when the daily quota is reached.
  • Clipboard: to copy a URL or the activity log only when you ask it to.

4. Sharing of data

The developer does not sell, rent, or transfer user data to third parties, and does not use it for advertising or profiling. The extension contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

5. Third-party services

The extension’s side panel loads the Cairo font from Google Fonts. As with any web font request, your IP address and basic request data may be sent to Google, and that processing is governed by Google’s privacy policy.

6. Retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until you delete it. You can erase everything with the “Clear all data” action inside the extension, or by removing the extension from chrome://extensions. The developer holds no copy of your data.

7. Security

The extension relies on Chrome’s built-in storage and permission model. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your device and browser updated and avoid sharing your device with untrusted people.

8. Children

The extension is intended for professionals working on websites and search optimization. It is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when new features are added. The date at the top of the page shows the last update, and material changes will be published here before they take effect.

10. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer via LinkedIn.
