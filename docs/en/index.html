Features

What the extension does

Every feature listed here exists in the current version of the extension.

01

Several sources, one queue

Sitemaps in XML, sitemap indexes and gzip files, pasted URLs, or an uploaded TXT, CSV or JSON file. Each load is added to the queue without clearing what is already there.

02

Smart duplicate removal

Duplicates inside the same batch, URLs already queued, and URLs requested before are filtered out. When a clean URL and a copy with extra parameters both appear, only the clean one is kept.

03

A mode for your goal

“New only” sends pages that are not indexed yet. “Re-index” refreshes pages that are already indexed. “Both” combines the two in one run.

04

Control over pacing

A configurable delay between requests, and a safe skip for URLs that do not fit. When Google’s daily quota is reached, the queue stops completely and affected URLs stay reserved until the quota resets.

05

Live tracking

Live counters for each status, search and filters, one-click URL copy, a full activity log, and CSV export with an encoding that displays Arabic correctly in Excel.

06

Safe stop on failure

The extension watches the Search Console tab. If the tab is closed or frozen, the queue stops safely, the active URL returns to waiting, and the log explains what happened.

How it works

Four steps, in the same order as the panel

01

Source

Paste a sitemap URL or a list of URLs, or upload a file with one URL per line.

02

Load

URLs are read, duplicates are removed, and the batch is added to the queue with a summary.

03

Settings

Choose the indexing mode and the delay between URLs. Your settings are saved on your device.

04

Index

Press “Start queue” and keep the Search Console tab open and signed in.

Privacy

Privacy is part of the design

IndexFlow needs no account and sends no data to the developer’s servers. Everything the extension needs is stored on your device. The full policy is available below.

  • Settings, queue and activity log stay in your browser.

FAQ

Common questions

Is IndexFlow free?

Yes. There is no paid plan and no hidden limit inside the extension.

Does it guarantee that pages will appear in search results?

No. IndexFlow only submits indexing requests. Whether and how a page is indexed or ranked is decided by Google.

Does the Search Console tab have to stay open?

Yes, and you must stay signed in. It is recommended to pin the tab and exclude it from Chrome’s memory saver so it does not freeze while the queue waits.

What happens when Google’s daily quota is reached?

The queue stops completely, and the affected URLs stay reserved until midnight, when the quota resets. Google sets the quota; the extension respects it.

Does the developer receive my data or my URLs?

No. Everything is kept inside your browser. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.
